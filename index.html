Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our conversion process and delivery guarantees.

"What if my game uses a completely custom or proprietary ruleset?"

If your game uses exploding dice, custom action points, or non-d20 mechanics, we run a Custom Schema Engineering Sprint. We build bespoke data models and mathematical verification gates specifically for your game system, guaranteeing 100% accurate VTT output across your entire catalog.

"What happens after I purchase a tier?"

Immediately after checkout, you receive an automated secure upload link to submit your PDF. Our pipeline processes your book within the stated turnaround window (24–48 hours), runs automated QA checks, and emails you a complete download package with your Foundry `.zip` module, JSON compendiums, and verification manifest.

"What if I have hundreds of backlist PDFs?"

Our Enterprise Backlist Vault handles up to 1,000 pages for $999. For multi-thousand-page catalog archives, our cloud workers process books in parallel with custom schema mapping and a formal SLA. Contact us directly for custom volume staging.

"Why not just copy-paste text into ChatGPT?"

Generative AI guesses missing stats, hallucinates spell mechanics, and destroys multi-column table hierarchies. VTT compendiums require rigid, schema-compliant JSON. We use spatial computer vision to isolate layouts and formal mathematical logic to verify every number before outputting ready-to-play files.

"How does the Free 1-Page Audit work?"

Send us your single hardest, messiest PDF page—dense multi-column text, intricate monster stat blocks, or nested tables. We will generate a verified Foundry VTT JSON file and a layout report completely free so you can test the quality firsthand before booking a project.
